AniTech Design System & Frontend Experience Specification
Overview
AniTech is not meant to feel like a normal anime streaming website. It is not supposed to feel like a random React dashboard with anime cards attached to it. It is not meant to imitate Crunchyroll, Netflix, or generic streaming clones directly. AniTech is designed to feel like a premium anime operating system. The interface should feel immersive, intelligent, elegant, and emotionally satisfying.
The core philosophy of AniTech is that anime fans spend hundreds or thousands of hours inside media applications. Therefore the interface itself becomes part of the experience. The product should feel like a place users want to stay inside, customize, explore, and emotionally connect with.
AniTech must combine:


premium streaming aesthetics

desktop application depth

media immersion

anime-inspired visual identity

modular productivity-style layouts

social interaction

personalization

cinematic motion

low-friction navigation

highly polished usability
The product must feel modern for years, not just trendy for one month.
